## What went wrong in the static version

The port image looked like Q4, the Marrakesh banner needed specialist knowledge, and the round was
decided only by the emblem.

## Concept — "A seat at a table of 150"

A membership story: rules for world trade (GATT 1947) → a new organisation (Marrakesh 1994,
1/1/1995, Genève) → Việt Nam applies (4/1/1995) → 11 years of preparation → approval (7/11/2006)
→ membership (11/1/2007). The pattern `3` is turned into an **elimination game among acronyms**.

## Acronym traps (all `3`)

| Trap | Distinguishing fact in the montage |
|---|---|
| FDI | B2: rules for trade, not capital; B6 "nộp đơn gia nhập" — one does not join FDI |
| FTA | B5: one organisation with a seat in Genève; B9 "thành viên thứ 150" |
| BTA | B7: the 2000 Việt Nam–US agreement is shown as one *step* |
| IMF | B3 GATT lineage (trade, not money); B5 founded 1995 (IMF 1945) |
| GATT (4 letters) | pattern ✗ → "its successor" is the intended inference |
