# Q5 — WTO · storyboard (M3.1 draft · PRIORITY ROUND)

> Contains the canonical answer — server/docs only. Pattern shown to players: **`3`** (one
> three-letter word). Source ids (`S-Q5-xx`) refer to [SOURCE-REGISTER](SOURCE-REGISTER.md).

## What went wrong in the static version

Container port → Marrakesh 1994 → signing in 2006 → emblem. The port opened on the same kind of
picture as Q4 (Hải Phòng ship), the Marrakesh banner needs specialist knowledge, and the round was
effectively decided only by the emblem — a picture-recognition test, not a reasoning chain.

## Concept — "A seat at a table of 150"

The round is a **membership story**: an organisation with rules for world trade, born 1995 out of a
1947 agreement, and Việt Nam's 12-year path from application (01/1995) to becoming the **150th
member (11/1/2007)**. The pattern `3` tells students from second 0 that the answer is a
**three-letter acronym**; the montage is designed as an *elimination game* among acronyms.

## Acronym traps (all `3`)

| Trap | Why tempting | Distinguishing fact used in the montage |
|---|---|---|
| **FDI** | Ports, factories, foreign capital | B1–B2 are about *rules for trade*, not capital; B6 "nộp đơn gia nhập" — you don't join FDI |
| **FTA** | "trade agreement", tariffs | B4–B5: one global organisation, HQ Genève; B9 "thành viên thứ 150" |
| **BTA** | Việt Nam–US 2000 | B7 shows BTA as a *step*, not the goal |
| **IMF** | International organisation, "membership" | B3 GATT lineage; trade, not money; B5 created 1995 (IMF 1945) |
| **ODA / GDP** | Economy vocabulary | No membership semantics |
| **GATT** | B3 | Pattern 4 ✗ → "the successor of GATT" is the inference we want |
