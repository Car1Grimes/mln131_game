## Concept — "Three circles that must overlap"

Economy alone (growth, smoke) → society (who benefits? poverty) → environment (cost and repair) →
the three circles with an empty centre → the 1987 definition (future generations) → the 2030
Agenda (three dimensions, balanced and integrated) → the 17-goal wheel. The word "bền vững" never
appears.

## Semantic-leakage rules

Forbidden before reveal: "phát triển bền vững", "bền vững", "Sustainable Development",
"sustainability", "SDG/SDGs", "ODD/ODS", "Mục tiêu Phát triển Bền vững", the full title of the 2030
Agenda, titles of QĐ 153/2004, QĐ 432/2012, QĐ 622/2017, the phrase "phát triển nhanh và bền
vững", the UN SDG logo wordmark and any icon set with words. Credit for the wheel during the round:
`Bánh xe 17 màu: Liên Hợp Quốc` — full credit, link and UN disclaimer only after the round.

## Pattern & traps

PHÁT TRIỂN BAO TRÙM is pattern-identical (`4·5·3·4`) → eliminated by environment + future
generations (B5–B7). Others (TĂNG TRƯỞNG XANH, KINH TẾ TUẦN HOÀN, BIẾN ĐỔI KHÍ HẬU, BẢO VỆ MÔI
TRƯỜNG) fail the pattern.
