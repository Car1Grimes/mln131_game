# Q6 — PHÁT TRIỂN BỀN VỮNG · storyboard (M3.1 draft · PRIORITY ROUND)

> Contains the canonical answer — server/docs only. Pattern shown to players: **`4·5·3·4`**.
> Source ids (`S-Q6-xx`) refer to [SOURCE-REGISTER](SOURCE-REGISTER.md).

## What went wrong in the static version

Smoke → slum vs skyline → wind/solar → Venn with "?". Three of four images read as
**environment**; students answered *bảo vệ môi trường*, *năng lượng tái tạo*, *biến đổi khí hậu*,
*kinh tế xanh*. The concept — **balancing economic, social and environmental dimensions for
present and future generations** — appeared only in the last picture.

## Concept — "Three circles that must overlap"

Each dimension gets its own short act, and each act shows the *cost of pursuing it alone*:

1. **Economy alone** → growth with smoke.
2. **Society** → growth that leaves people behind; then poverty reduction.
3. **Environment** → climate risk; then repair (renewables).
4. **Integration** → the three circles overlap; the 1987 definition introduces **time**
   (future generations); the 2030 Agenda makes it a global programme; Việt Nam's own strategy line.

The centre of the Venn is the answer; the word "**bền vững**" never appears on screen.

## Pattern & traps

`4·5·3·4` = "PHÁT TRIỂN ___ ____" is quickly guessed once students think "phát triển …".
The real trap is a **pattern-identical** answer: **PHÁT TRIỂN BAO TRÙM** (inclusive development,
`4·5·3·4`). The montage must therefore stress **environment + future generations**, which
inclusive development does not centre on. Other traps: *TĂNG TRƯỞNG XANH* (`4·6·4` ✗),
*KINH TẾ TUẦN HOÀN* (`4·2·4·4` ✗), *BIẾN ĐỔI KHÍ HẬU* (`4·3·3·3` ✗), *BẢO VỆ MÔI TRƯỜNG* (`3·2·3·5` ✗).

## Text hazards (validator + common sense)

Forbidden on screen anywhere (even split by other words is a giveaway): "phát triển bền vững",
"bền vững", "Sustainable Development", "sustainability", "SDG(s)", Vietnamese SDG icon sets, the
full title of the 2030 Agenda ("…vì sự phát triển bền vững"), the title of QĐ 622/2017 or
QĐ 153/2004, and the Party phrase "phát triển nhanh và bền vững". Use **"Chương trình nghị sự
2030"**, **"17 mục tiêu toàn cầu"**, **"Báo cáo Tương lai chung của chúng ta"**.
