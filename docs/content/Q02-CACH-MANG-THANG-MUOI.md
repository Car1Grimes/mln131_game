## Concept — "One city, one year, three uprisings"

Petrograd 1917: February → July → October. A recurring **two-calendar motif** shows each date as
`lịch cũ` (Julian, used in Russia until 1918) and `lịch mới` (Gregorian). The round ends by
showing the Old-Style date of the uprising — the month that gives the event its name.

**Date notation used on screen:** `25/10/1917 (lịch cũ) = 7/11/1917 (lịch mới)`. In 1917 the
Julian calendar was 13 days behind.

## Why the pattern matters

`4·4·5·4` fits "CÁCH MẠNG THÁNG ____" with a 4-letter month → MƯỜI. *THÁNG HAI / TÁM / BẢY* are
`…·3`. So the first 15 s must not say "cách mạng".
