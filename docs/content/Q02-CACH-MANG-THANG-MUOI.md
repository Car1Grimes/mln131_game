# Q2 — CÁCH MẠNG THÁNG MƯỜI · storyboard (M3.1 draft)

> Contains the canonical answer — server/docs only. Pattern shown to players: **`4·4·5·4`**.
> Source ids (`S-Q2-xx`) refer to [SOURCE-REGISTER](SOURCE-REGISTER.md).

## Concept — "One city, one year, three uprisings"

Petrograd, 1917. The montage walks through the three upheavals of that year — February, July,
October — and asks students to identify the one that "changed the world" and is studied as the
start of the epoch of transition to socialism. A recurring **calendar motif** (a tear-off calendar
showing two dates, Old Style / New Style) carries the narrative and explains why an "October"
revolution is commemorated on 7 November.

Narrative arc: hunger & war → the Tsar falls → shooting in July → night of 24–25 October →
first decrees → the light reaches Việt Nam (Hồ Chí Minh) → a Soviet stamp says the name in Russian.

## Why the pattern matters here

`4·4·5·4` fits "CÁCH MẠNG THÁNG ____" with a 4-letter month: only **MƯỜI** (and the non-event
"CHÍN") fit. *CÁCH MẠNG THÁNG HAI / TÁM / BẢY* are `…·3` and are eliminated by the pattern alone.
Therefore the first 15 s must **not** say "cách mạng": it would make the round trivial. The
opening stays on war, hunger and a city without naming events.
