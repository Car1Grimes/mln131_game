## Concept — "A turning point"

Crisis numbers → reforms that started *before* 1986 → Congress VI (15–18/12/1986) and its
self-critical line → first results → the masked phrase "đổi mới tư duy". Market activity and
reform experiments are shown as existing before the Congress; the Congress is presented as the
point where a **comprehensive line** was adopted, not the start of all markets.
