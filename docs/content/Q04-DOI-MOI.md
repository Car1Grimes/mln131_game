## Concept — "A turning point"

Crisis → experiments from below → a decision at the top → visible consequences. The montage shows
that **Đổi Mới was a comprehensive policy line adopted by Đại hội VI (12/1986)**, prepared by
earlier local and partial reforms (Chỉ thị 100 in 1981, the 1985 price–wage–money reform), and
followed by laws and results (1987 foreign-investment law, 1988 Khoán 10, 1989 rice exports).

Editorial cautions from the brief:
- Market activity did **not** begin only in 1986 → B4 shows 1981 and "phá rào" before the Congress.
- 1980s *reconstructions* are labelled; the only archival 1986 image is the Congress hall.

## Pattern & cross-round trap

`3·3` = same as **BAO CẤP** (Q1). Students who have just played Q1 will see 1980s hardship at the
start and may think "the answer is the opposite of Q1". That is fine — it is a *reasoned* path —
but the opening must remain ambiguous between *crisis* words (LẠM PHÁT `3·4`, KHỦNG HOẢNG `5·5`)
and the reform itself. Other `3·3`-compatible guesses: *MỞ CỬA* (`2·3` ✗), *HỘI NHẬP* (`3·4` ✗),
*CẢI CÁCH* (`4·4` ✗). The pattern therefore helps a lot from ~30 s; this round's difficulty
comes from the first 20 s being about the *problem*, not the solution.
