## C-1 · Reveal table wraps team names on narrow phones

- **Evidence:** [reveal-q1-phone360.jpg](media-review/m33b/reveal-q1-phone360.jpg) — 360×740 viewport:
  the six-column reveal table (Thứ tự · Đội · Câu trả lời · Thời điểm · Kết quả · Điểm) breaks "Đội 1"
  into "Đội / 1" and "Câu trả lời" / "Không nộp" over two lines.
- **Where:** `src/components/RevealTable.tsx`, `.mp-table` styles in `src/styles.css`.
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  1. At 360×740 and 390×844 no team name ("Đội 1" … "Đội 5", and a 40-character custom name truncated
     with an ellipsis) breaks inside the name; the table has no horizontal page scroll.
  2. Order, team, answer, result and points remain visible for all five rows without zooming; "Thời
     điểm" may move into a secondary line or be hidden on phones, never the points.
  3. Projector (1920×1080) and laptop (1366×768) layouts unchanged (screenshot comparison).
  4. An E2E measurement (as in `e2e/presentation.mjs`) asserts: each `.reveal-table` team-name cell is a
     single line box at 360 px; `documentElement.scrollWidth ≤ innerWidth`.

## C-2 · Final title wraps "điểm" onto its own line

- **Evidence:** [final-phone360.jpg](media-review/m33b/final-phone360.jpg) — "Đội 3: hạng 3 · 120" on one
  line and "điểm" alone on the next.
- **Where:** `FinalScreen` in `src/player/PlayerApp.tsx` (`.final-title`).
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  1. The score and its unit never separate ("120 điểm" kept together, e.g. a non-breaking space or a
     no-wrap span); no single-word last line for the 5 default team names at 360 and 390 px widths.
  2. Title font size never below 16 CSS px; no horizontal scroll.
  3. Test: at 360×740 the text node "điểm" shares a line box with the score digits for all 5 teams.

## C-3 · Projector confetti briefly crosses the final title

- **Evidence:** [final-projector.jpg](media-review/m33b/final-projector.jpg) — confetti pieces over
  "Đồng hạng nhất: Đội 1 & Đội 2" and the eyebrow during the first seconds of the final screen.
- **Where:** `src/components/Confetti.tsx`, `.confetti` (z-index 5) vs `.final-title`.
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  1. Over the first 6 s of the final screen, no confetti piece's box intersects the bounding boxes of the
     eyebrow, the final title or the podium names/scores (sampled every animation frame in an E2E test),
     **or** the title layer sits above the confetti with ≥ 4.5:1 contrast maintained.
  2. Celebration is kept (confetti still visible around the podium); `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`
     still hides confetti.
  3. No confetti when nobody scored (unchanged; covered by the no-winner fixture).
