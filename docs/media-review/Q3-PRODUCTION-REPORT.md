## 8. Storyboard departures, legal and rights notes (for review)

1. **B6:** the commune photo q03-03 (no permission) is replaced by an own animated illustration of
   equal votes with unequal capital (c20) — clearer for the concept than a meeting photo.
2. **B5 card 3:** both statutory conditions (capital or voting shares), see §9.
3. **B5 layout:** cards shown one after another (full width, ≥ 16 px on phones) instead of a 2×2 grid.
4. **B3 caption:** "28 người cùng mở một cửa hàng" (was "28 người lao động mở chung một cửa hàng") to
   fit one line at phone size; the museum photo is cropped to avoid the "COOP" advert.
5. **B7:** heading "Luật năm 2023 — Điều 61, đại hội toàn thể"; the delegate-assembly sentence is no
   longer on screen (phone overflow) — the statement stays accurate because its scope is named.
   **Lecturer check suggested.**
6. **B10 recap:** illustration → rice field → signboard (museum photo dropped for size).
7. **Legal scope:** card 4 / e8 describe the *full assembly* rule (approved); a delegate assembly
   weights delegates' votes by the members they represent (Đ.61 k.4, second sentence) — not shown.
8. **Rights:** Đ.188 was read from a gazette facsimile on a third-party host (part 2 of VBHN 67 was
   not found on the official CDN). No post-round credits screen yet (open item from earlier rounds).
9. **Performance:** no exception (largest clip 588 KB, ≈ 2.1 s on 2.5 Mbit/s).
